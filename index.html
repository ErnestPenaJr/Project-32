Welcome Message

Welcome to the DoCM Office Space Reservation Calendar. Use this tool to manage your room bookings efficiently and stay organized.

Overview

This page allows you to view available office spaces, reserve rooms, and manage your bookings using a calendar interface.

IT Assistance

The Division of Cancer Medicine maintains the DoCM Reservation System. If you have any questions or concerns regarding DoCM Reservation System, Contact information: 4-INFO Call Center at ext. 713-794-4636 or email 4info@mdanderson.org

Making a Booking
  • Click the "New Booking" button in the top-right corner to open the booking form.
  • Select a room from the dropdown menu.
  • Enter the start and end times for your booking.
  • Optionally, select a recurring schedule (daily, weekly, or monthly).
  • Click "Book Room" to finalize your booking.
Booking for Someone Else
  • By default the reservation is recorded for you, so booking for yourself needs no extra steps.
  • To reserve a space on a colleague's behalf, tick "I am booking this for someone else" in the booking form and enter their name. Their email and department are optional but helpful.
  • Both names are kept: the request still records that you submitted it, and shows Reservation For separately. Approvers see both, and the confirmation email lists them.
  • Reservations made before this field existed simply show the requester as the person the space is for.
Viewing Booking History
  • Navigate to the "My Bookings" section from the top navigation bar.
  • View all your past and upcoming bookings.
  • Click on any booking to see detailed information.
Using the Calendar
  • View bookings for all rooms or filter by specific rooms using the dropdown menu.
  • Click on a booking to view details or manage it (if you are the creator or an admin).
  • Navigate through different calendar views: day, week, or month.
  • Hover over bookings to see quick details in a tooltip.
  • Opening a booking shows its full record: request number, who requested it, who it is for, the room and location, date and time, recurrence, purpose, submission date and current status. Anything not filled in is simply left out.
Cancel a Booking
  • Click the booking on the calendar, then choose "Cancel Booking".
  • You may enter a reason. It is optional, but it is stored with the reservation and included in the notice sent to the requester, so it is worth adding.
  • Confirm by clicking "Yes, cancel it".
  • The requester is notified of the cancellation — including the request number, room, date and time, who cancelled it and the reason if one was given. If you cancelled your own booking, that notice goes to you.
  • You can only cancel your own reservations unless you are an administrator.
